## Decision question

Does the complete published Soft Anisotropic Diagrams (SAD) system expose a material
representation/convergence gap in StructSplat when both finish with the same number of learned
exported scalar coordinates,
or is the reported SAD advantage explained by its nominal rate convention, unmatched state, or
missing cold-render parity?

The null is that pinned native SAD does not clear the frozen quality, convergence, and performance
gates against the strongest already-frozen ordinary StructSplat field. A null result closes SAD
reuse for this repository. A pass authorizes exactly one later mechanism assay of learned reach,
learned temperature, and explicit top-8 distance ownership as an inseparable block. It does not
authorize a production renderer, an alpha-0.7 allocator, a default, or a compression claim.

## Prior art and irreducible contribution

The method is SAD itself ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21984),
[official implementation](https://github.com/LuckyIYI/SAD)); the upstream repository is therefore
dispositive method prior art. Apollonius diagrams, softmax partitions, top-K mixture rendering,
jump flooding, Image-GS, RBF/meshfree approximants, and split/prune optimization occupy the
components. BENCH-016 contributes only a source-pinned, centrally scored recipient comparison and
an evidence decision about StructSplat's binding bottleneck (novelty class `N0`).

The pinned upstream commit is
`0eeb7e1e72b81e550f90db8ebbf432cdc57383ed`. Use its CUDA backend and its committed
`training_config.json` unchanged. The native operator is

```text
G_i = exp(a_i) u_i u_i^T + exp(-a_i) (I - u_i u_i^T)
s_i(x) = sqrt((x-p_i)^T G_i (x-p_i))/L - r_i/L
w_i(x) = softmax_{i in K8(x)}(-exp(log_tau_i) s_i(x))
y(x) = sum_i w_i(x) c_i
```

where the upstream system owns gradient initialization, 4,000 updates, candidate propagation,
Hilbert probes/random injection, densification, exact removal-delta pruning, and the fixed `K=8`
candidate map. Testing only its responsibility-density alpha is prohibited: FIT-018 already
rejected that isolated transplant.

## Frozen arms and budgets

Run nominal SAD rates `{0.5, 2.0}` bits/pixel. Upstream defines its target by `16 bytes/site`; keep
this as `sad_nominal_bpp` and never call it a complete stream. Record final active site count `N`
from each native row.

For each `image x rate` run three arms. Every arm has three fresh-process execution repetitions
indexed `{0,1,2}` with the same frozen method seed; these are repeated executions, not independent
random seeds, and no repetition may be selected or discarded by outcome:

1. `sad_native`: upstream CUDA at the commit/config above, gradient init, 4,000 steps,
   `--target-bpp`, and `--log-freq 20`. No upstream source or JSON change is allowed.
2. `ss_same_n` (descriptive): StructSplat's frozen `structsplat_best_checkpoint` ordinary
   normalized constant-RGB method at final count `N`, seed 0, 4,000 steps, exact CUDA renderer,
   L1 plus `0.3` SSIM, feature cap `12@160`, `log_every=20`, and full-count best-PSNR checkpoint
   selection. This cadence is part of the method because it controls the trace, checkpoint
   candidates, and checkpoint-render overhead.
3. `ss_equal_terminal_coordinates` (primary): the identical StructSplat method at
   `ceil(10*N/8)` Gaussians. SAD has ten trainable floats/site and the StructSplat constant field
   has eight floats/Gaussian; this arm matches the terminal learned/exported coordinate count, not
   the transient training state, optimizer state, allocated capacity, FLOPs, or memory.

The adapter must obtain `N` from every completed native repeat. If repeat final counts differ, use
the maximum count across the three repeats to schedule both conservative StructSplat controls;
otherwise use their shared count. Each StructSplat repetition must start with
`max(16, round(0.5 * final_count))` Gaussians and use exactly five tensor-residual growth events;
set `split_every=floor(4000/6)=666`, `split_count=ceil((final_count-start_count)/5)`, cap every
event at `final_count`, preserve the frozen method's `split_schedule_stops_at_max=False`, and
require both the selected and terminal field counts to equal that cap. The first five schedule
opportunities must add rows and reach the cap; the sixth opportunity at step 3,996 is the frozen
cap no-op and is not a growth event.
Before fitting, require `D=final_count-start_count >= 21`. After fitting, require exactly five
persisted split events with positive `added` at completed steps `{666,1332,1998,2664,3330}`
(zero-based history iterations `{665,1331,1997,2663,3329}`), require their additions to sum to
`D`, and require every resolved `FitConfig` to retain `log_every=20`.
It may not select between StructSplat methods or repetitions per image. Early stopping, QAT,
affine colors, opacity, BENCH-015 lifting, and adaptive extra capacity are disabled. Image-GS
may be reported later as a separately pinned external diagnostic, but it is not a required arm and
cannot affect this decision.

Let `N_target=floor(sad_target_bpp * H * W / 128 + 0.5)`, matching positive C++ `std::round`.
Record achieved nominal rate
`128*N/(H*W)` for every native repetition and require
`abs(N-N_target) <= max(1, ceil(0.01*N_target))`; otherwise that requested-rate cell is invalid.
The rate labels always mean requested-rate commands, while tables must show the achieved rates.

## Frozen analysis and decision

All sixteen `image x rate` cells, all 48 native repetitions, and all 96 StructSplat repetitions must
be valid. Analyze the two rates separately. Average each arm's three scalar quality metrics and
aligned traces within image/rate before pairing arms; use each arm's median repetition time and
memory before forming per-image ratios. Compute a deterministic 10,000-resample image-cluster
bootstrap with NumPy `Generator(PCG64(0))` and linear quantiles for median/mean paired effects.

Trace time has explicit update semantics. A native stdout row labelled iteration `k` is the
post-update state at progress `(k+1)/4000`; its final post-update reconstruction is the endpoint at
`1.0`. A StructSplat history row labelled `k` is the pre-update state at progress `k/4000`; append
the separately recorded trajectory-terminal, post-update reconstruction at `1.0`. The selected
best checkpoint is used for final quality but must never replace the terminal trajectory endpoint.
Linearly interpolate both traces on their union over the common closed interval
`[1/4000, 1]`, do not extrapolate or hold outside it, integrate trapezoidally, and divide by the
interval length. Preserve SAD stdout's `0.01 dB` trace precision as a limitation.

SAD passes this development screen only if **at each rate**, relative to
`ss_equal_terminal_coordinates`:

1. median paired PSNR gain is at least `+1.0 dB`;
2. at least `7/8` images have positive PSNR gain and the worst gain is at least `-0.25 dB`;
3. the 95% image-bootstrap lower bound for mean PSNR gain is strictly positive;
4. median LPIPS is non-worse (`median(LPIPS_SAD - LPIPS_SS) <= 0`);
5. median **internal-training-state normalized-iteration** PSNR AUC gain is at least `+0.5 dB`;
6. median native training wall-time ratio is at most `0.50`;
7. median prepared render-only time ratio is at most `1.10`;
8. neither median peak VRAM nor peak RSS exceeds `1.50x` the control.

Internal-training-state normalized-iteration AUC is the trapezoidal mean of the logged training
state's PSNR over progress `iteration/4000`, with linear interpolation on the union of logged
progress points. For SAD this is the upstream training-state trace, not primary saved-TXT replay
quality. It measures internal optimization-trajectory shape, not recipient quality at intermediate
iterations or wall-time convergence; wall time is deliberately a separate systems gate. Missing
LPIPS or memory is a failed gate, not an imputed value. The same-N comparison, nominal/actual
bytes, central MS-SSIM, cold timing, and repeat variability are descriptive guardrails.

If every gate passes, the next action is `implement one controlled operator bridge`. If any gate
fails, the next action is `abandon SAD reuse`. Do not rescue a failure by changing images, resize,
rates, upstream schedule, count conversion, StructSplat arm, horizon, metrics, or thresholds.

## Claim boundary by axis

- **Quality:** tested only on this eight-image downsampled development screen.
- **Convergence:** tested as a whole-system 4,000-update trajectory; not yet attributed to the
  renderer, initialization, allocation, or optimizer.
- **Performance:** tested on one RTX 3050/software environment; no cross-GPU claim.
- **Compression:** not tested; saved TXT plus pinned decoder/config is recipient-replayable only,
  never deployable, self-contained, or a compressed representation.
- **Expressiveness:** a quality pass at conservatively at-least-matched terminal learned/exported
  coordinates is evidence of a whole-system representation/optimization gap, not equal training
  state, a rank theorem, or an isolated primitive result.
