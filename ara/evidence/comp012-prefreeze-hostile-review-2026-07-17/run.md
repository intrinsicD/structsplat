# COMP-012 hostile pre-freeze review

**Date:** 2026-07-17
**Scope:** design review only; no task freeze, implementation, target access, or experiment result
**Verdict:** `NO_GO_REPAIR_BEFORE_FREEZE`

## Blocking corrections

1. Bind the assay to genuinely new images selected by a target-blind hash rule, excluding every
   prior COMP-008/COMP-011 development and confirmation image. Reserve a separate unopened
   confirmation roster.
2. Freeze one primary endpoint. The proposed primary is the target-guided fine-alphabet terminal
   under the incumbent complete-stream cap; diagnostic or transient states cannot rescue it.
3. Equal cap is not equal rate. To attribute a benefit to alphabet release, continue the fine
   search under the exact terminal coarse-stream byte count and compare at equal-or-lower bytes.
   The incumbent-Q comparison measures common-cap headroom only.
4. A nested coarse-to-fine path is guaranteed nonworse on its internal CPU objective. Add a direct
   fine-alphabet start with the complete neighborhood and a target-blind strongest uniform-lattice
   control. Select that lattice by minimum frozen source-teacher distortion among feasible
   candidates, with deterministic byte/step/hash ties.
5. Freeze independent-step-3 infeasibility as a valid method failure. Keep the header, color-range
   float bits, geometry, row order, and every non-RGB symbol/payload authority bitwise unchanged.
6. Exhaust every scalar proposal in a terminal zero-accept sweep, recompute the render and stream
   exactly, persist visited-state hashes, and claim only stationarity for the frozen CPU clamped
   objective and neighborhood.
7. The size oracle must recompute changed-channel empirical normalization, exact arithmetic length,
   and the whole five-channel compressed model. Prove size-only/full-encode equality on fixtures
   and accepted/final states, then persist, cold-decode, hash, and canonically re-encode the blob.
8. Run source arms in target-denied workers and seal them before target scoring. Target arms may use
   the target as legal per-image encoder input only after roster, configuration, order, starts,
   caps, and arms are sealed.
9. Transfer the primary result to conservative repeated CUDA PSNR with MS-SSIM and LPIPS guards.
   Internal CPU target-SSE descent is insufficient because part of the nested improvement is
   guaranteed by construction.
10. Runtime includes footprint construction, proposals, size queries, sweeps, and final encode.
    Runtime-killed partial states cannot be called converged or used for quality promotion.
11. Treat images, not tuples or edits, as independent units; analyze tuples separately. A
    development result cannot establish held-out confirmation, fitter convergence, renderer speed,
    global optimality, representation expressiveness, or state of the art.

## Novelty boundary

Nearest prior art already covers decoder-compatible quantized-index optimization, hard target-rate
allocation, coordinate-descent quantization, perceptual RDO, learned-encoder test-time
optimization, and renderer-aware Gaussian RDO. A defensible contribution would therefore be the
StructSplat-specific operational combination and evidence: exact complete self-identifying bytes
per accepted edit, a frozen strongest-incumbent cap, unchanged SSP2F decoding, exact clamped
renderer distortion, causal source/target/equal-rate controls, and cold replay on multiple new
images. It would not be a generic RDO or coordinate-descent novelty claim.

## Consequence

The exact-Q direction remains worth testing, but the initial summarized COMP-012 protocol must not
be frozen or implemented as written. The repaired preregistration must incorporate the controls
above before any new development target is opened.
